# How do I change an appointment's status from the schedule?

1. Hover over the appointment in the schedule.
2. Select the **Change intervention status** icon (the triangle).
3. Select **Book**, **Confirm**, **Unplan**, **Reactivate**, **Suspend**, or **Delete**.

Select **Back** to close the menu without a change. Check the appointment before you use **Delete**.
