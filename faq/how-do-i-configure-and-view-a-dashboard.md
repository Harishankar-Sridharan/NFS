# How do I configure and view a dashboard?

1. Go to **Supervision > Dashboard > Configure**. On the four tabs (**Human resources**, **Period**, **Activities**, and **Other**), enter your criteria, then select **Validate**.
2. Go to **Supervision > Dashboard > Appointments**. In **Display by**, choose the grouping (for example **Activity type**, **Status**, or **Resource**). To add a second level, enable **Detail by**. Then select **Apply**.

Schedule summaries, objectives, analyses, and the **Edit dashboard** entry follow the same pattern. Most of them offer an **Export csv** button.
