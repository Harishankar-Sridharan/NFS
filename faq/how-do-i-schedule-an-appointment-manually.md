# How do I schedule an appointment manually?

Use **Manual scheduling** when you create an appointment, or **Manual rescheduling** from a scheduled appointment record.

1. Select the field worker.
2. Enter the **Date / Time**, then choose **exact** or **from**.
3. Select **Continue**.

If a constraint is not met, messages appear at the top of the screen. Read them before you use **Force appointment**.
