# How do I change what the schedule displays?

In the schedule's top bar, choose:

* a **Calendar type**: **Interventions**, **Workload**, **Position**, or **Intervention type**
* a **Period**: **Day** or **Week**
* a **Level**: **Worksite**, **Sector**, **Team**, **Field worker**, **Region**, or **Multi-resources**

Then select the item to display on the right. Use the arrows beside the period to move between periods.
