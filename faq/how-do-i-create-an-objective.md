# How do I create an objective?

1. Go to **Administration > Company data > Objectives**.
2. Choose the tab that matches the level: **Human resource**, **Worksite**, or **Resource type**.
3. Select **Create an objective**, enter the **Region** and the item, and choose the objective **Type**.
4. Enter the **From** / **To** period and the **Value**, then select **Save**.

Completion statuses and follow-up actions are created under **Activity monitoring**, and typologies under **Typology**.
