# How do I create an on-call week template?

1. Create the on-call day templates first, under **Administration > Company data > On-call duties > Day template**.
2. Go to **Week template** and select **Create an on-call week template**.
3. Enter **Name** and **Region**, then choose the **Start day** and **Transfer time**.
4. Select a day template for each day in the table.
5. Select **Save**.
