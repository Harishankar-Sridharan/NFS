# What does the Supervision module contain?

Open the module from **Application launcher > Supervision**. The left menu offers:

* **Dashboard**, **Supervisor home**, and **Log**
* a **Dashboard** group: **Configure**, **Appointments**, **Schedule summary by day**, **Schedule summary by week**, and **Edit dashboard**
* several analyses, such as **Appointment scheduling quality**, **Objectives**, and **Overtime**
