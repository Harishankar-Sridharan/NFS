# How is the Planning screen organized?

The screen has three panels: the workspace on the left (shortcuts, search forms, and records), the map at the top right, and the field-worker schedule at the bottom right.

Shortcuts include **BOOK A NEW APPOINTMENT**, **SEARCH FOR APPOINTMENTS**, and **SEARCH FOR CUSTOMERS**.

Select **Planning** next to the logo to open the module menu. Its sections are **Appointments**, **Customers**, **Planning**, **Location**, and **Exports**. **Alerts** and **View managers** are at the bottom.
