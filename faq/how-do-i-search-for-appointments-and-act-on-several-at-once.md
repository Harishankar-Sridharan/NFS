# How do I search for appointments and act on several at once?

1. Go to **Planning > Appointments menu > Search for appointments**, or use the **SEARCH FOR APPOINTMENTS** shortcut.
2. Enter criteria, then select **Search**. Entering at least a date or a status keeps results manageable.
3. Select the appointments you need, then choose a bulk action below the list, such as **Export to CSV**, **Announce**, **Edit dates**, **Edit statuses**, **Schedule**, or **Subcontract**.

Select **•••** at the start of a row to open that appointment's record.
