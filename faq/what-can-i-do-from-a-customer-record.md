# What can I do from a customer record?

The buttons below the customer information are **Create an appointment**, **Add to selected customer list**, **Search nearby**, **List appointments**, **Propagate updates**, **Weekly calendar**, and **Monthly calendar**.

To review appointments, select **List appointments**, adjust the **Status**, **From**, and **To** filters, then select **Search**. The page shows indicators such as **Number of appointments** and **Total mileage** for the period.
