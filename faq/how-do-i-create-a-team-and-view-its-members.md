# How do I create a team and view its members?

1. Go to **Administration > Company data > Team**, then select **Create a team**.
2. Enter the **Name** and select the **Service region**, both required.
3. Optionally complete **Abbreviation**, **Description**, and **Time zone**, then select **Save**.

Select the **Members** tab to see the team's members, and use the **Add** button beneath the list to add them.
