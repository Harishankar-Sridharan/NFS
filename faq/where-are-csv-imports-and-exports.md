# Where are CSV imports and exports?

Go to **Administration > CSV Files**. The group includes **Import a CSV File** and **Export a CSV File**.

Other screens also export CSV: the appointment search (**Export to CSV**), the customer search (**Export**), the Supervision analyses (**Export csv**), and MySearch lists (which export the columns in the view's order).
