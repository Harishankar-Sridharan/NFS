# What does an appointment record show, and how do I edit it?

The record is organized into **General**, **Customer**, **Communication**, **Completion status**, **Additional data**, **Comments**, **Messages**, and **History** sections.

The buttons at the bottom depend on the status. Scheduled appointments show buttons such as **Reschedule**, **Manual rescheduling**, **Complete...**, **Split...**, **Copy**, and **Subcontract**.

To edit an appointment, select **Edit...**, update the fields in the **Edit record**, then select **Save**.
