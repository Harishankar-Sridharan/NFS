# FAQ overview

This FAQ answers common questions about each module of the Nomadia Field Service back-office.

### Feature overview

The FAQ covers these areas:

* **Introduction**: Signing in, navigation, and appointment statuses.
* **Portal**: The home dashboard and its indicators.
* **Planning**: The schedule, MySearch views, and resource monitoring.
* **Appointments**: Scheduling, searching for, and editing appointments.
* **Customers**: Creating, searching for, and using customer records.
* **Unavailabilities**: Declaring and searching for unavailabilities.
* **Availabilities**: Resource availability schedules.
* **Supervision**: Appointment tracking, the log, and dashboards.
* **Fulfilment**: Comparing planned and completed days.
* **Report**: Report templates and form templates.
* **Administration**: Company data, optimization, mobility, users, customization, and tools.
* **Imports and exports**: CSV imports and exports.
