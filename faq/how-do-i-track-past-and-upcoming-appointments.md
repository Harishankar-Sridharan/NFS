# How do I track past and upcoming appointments?

Go to **Supervision > Supervisor home**. The page has two tables, week by week:

* **Number of appointments** shows **Candidates**, **Scheduled**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled** appointments by group or intervention type.
* **Revenue in k€** shows **Requested**, **Confirmed**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled** amounts.

The **Cumulative** column totals each row, and the **Total** row totals the weeks.
