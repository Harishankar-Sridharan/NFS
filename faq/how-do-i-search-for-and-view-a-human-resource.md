# How do I search for and view a human resource?

1. Go to **Administration > Company data > Human resource**.
2. Enter criteria such as **Last name**, **First name**, **Region**, or **Team**, then select **Search**.
3. Select a row to open the record. It has tabs including **Information**, **Assignment**, **User**, **Typical week**, **Limits**, and **Skills**.

Use **Create a human resource** to add a new one, and **Save** to save changes to the record.
