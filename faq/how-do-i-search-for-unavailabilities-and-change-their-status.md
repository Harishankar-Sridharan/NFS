# How do I search for unavailabilities and change their status?

1. Go to **Planning > Planning menu > Search for unavailabilities**.
2. Enter criteria such as **Type**, **Resource**, **Team**, or **Status**, and the **From** and **To** dates in **Date**.
3. Select **Search**.

The results show the **External reference**, **Type**, **Resource**, **Status**, and **Date / Time**. Use **Change statuses** below the table to change statuses, and **Reset** to clear the criteria.
