# How do I create a vehicle and assign it to a field worker?

1. Go to **Administration > Mobility > Vehicles**, then select **Create a vehicle**.
2. On the **Information** tab, enter the **Name** and **Region**, complete other fields if needed, then select **Save**.
3. To assign it, go to **Administration > Mobility > Assignments** and select **New assignment**.
4. Choose the **Field worker** and the **Vehicle** or **Tracking equipment**, with the assignment period.
