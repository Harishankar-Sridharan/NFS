# How do I sign in and sign out?

### Sign in

1. Open the Nomadia Field Service address in your browser.
2. Under **Please identify yourself:**, enter your username, then your password.
3. Select **Sign in**. If your organization uses single sign-on, select **Sign in with SAML** instead.

### Sign out

Select the user icon at the top right, then select **Sign out**. You can also use the **Sign out** button in the Portal.
