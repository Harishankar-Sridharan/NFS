# How do I configure a MySearch view and show it on the home screen?

1. Open **Planning > Planning menu > View managers**, or use **Edit MySearch** from a results list.
2. In the five-step wizard, set the **Filters** (at least one criterion), **Columns** (at least one), **Optional actions**, and **Bulk actions**.
3. On **Proceed**, enter a **Name** and check **Result list max size** (default 300). To show the view as a card, enable **Show counter**.
4. Select **Save changes**, then close the window.

To show a view on the home screen, select or clear its **Home page** checkbox, then select **Save changes** at the top of the page.
