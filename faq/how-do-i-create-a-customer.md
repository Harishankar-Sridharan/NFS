# How do I create a customer?

1. Go to **Planning > Customers menu > Create a customer**.
2. On the **Details** tab, enter the **Company name** (required).
3. On the **Address** tab, enter the **Address**, **Postcode** (required), **City** (required), and **Country**. Select **Validate address**, then choose the matching address in the **Address search** window.
4. Complete the other tabs if needed: **Contacts**, **Project**, **Assignments**, **Schedule**, and **Preferences**.
5. Select **Save**. The **Saved successfully...** message confirms the record. New buttons appear, including **Create an appointment**.
