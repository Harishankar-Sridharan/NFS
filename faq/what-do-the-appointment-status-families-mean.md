# What do the appointment status families mean?

Statuses are grouped into families:

* **Candidate**: Requested, Suspended
* **Scheduled**: Planned, Booked, Confirmed
* **Completed**: Completed, Moved, Unplanned
* **Cancelled**: Abandoned, Unscheduled
* **Subcontracted**: Subcontracted

While you book an appointment, the **Scheduling in progress** status appears until the selected time slot is confirmed.
