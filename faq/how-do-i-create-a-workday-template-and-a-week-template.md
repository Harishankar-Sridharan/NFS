# How do I create a workday template and a week template?

1. Go to **Administration > Company Data > Calendars > Day Template** and select **Create workday template**.
2. Enter the **Name** and **Region**, the **Working hours**, the **Lunch break** range, and the **Break duration**, then select **Save**.
3. For a week template, go to **Week Template**, select **Create workweek template**, and select a day template for each day of the week.

Create day templates before week templates. Public holidays and sales periods are under **Calendars** too.
