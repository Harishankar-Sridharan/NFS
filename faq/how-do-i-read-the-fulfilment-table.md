# How do I read the Fulfilment table?

* The left side shows the plan: **No.**, **Stage**, **Address**, **Scheduled on**, **from**, **to**, **Duration**, and **Driving duration**.
* The right side shows the completion: **No.**, **Completed**, **Start time**, **End time**, **Duration**, **Driving duration**, and **Status**.

Stages include **Start of day**, **Break**, numbered appointments, and **End of day**. The **Status** column shows coloured labels such as **Received** or **Sent**.
