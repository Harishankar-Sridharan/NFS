# How do I monitor a field worker's workload?

### Route indicators

In the resource mini-card:

* The **ROUTES** tab shows appointment count, kilometres, travel time, average speed, appointment duration, available time, and time reserve.
* The **QUOTA** tab (disabled by default) shows the route ratio, fill rate, and any quantity quotas.
* The **VEHICLE** tab shows maximum quantities.

These tabs are set in **Administration > Customization > Application configuration** under **hrInfo**.

### Activity table

Display the schedule, then select the **Activity table** icon (the chart icon) in the toolbar. It compares field workers' visits, visit and travel durations, kilometres, fill rate, route ratio, and vehicle.

Permission `CALLCENTER_PLANNING_ACTIVITY_BOARD` is required to display it. Permission `CALLCENTER_PLANNING_ACTIVITY_BOARD_CONFIGURATION` is required to configure its columns.
