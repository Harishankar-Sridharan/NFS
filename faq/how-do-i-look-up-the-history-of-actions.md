# How do I look up the history of actions?

1. Go to **Supervision > Log**.
2. On the **Log configuration** page, choose the **Select a business data type** value: **All**, **Appointments**, **Unavailabilities**, or **Exceptional locations**.
3. Set the resource scope, customer and identifier filters, and the period if needed.
4. Select **Validate**.

The **Search result** page shows the **Type**, **Identifier**, **Modification date and time**, **Author**, and **Action** of each entry.
