# How do I read attendance, activity, and forecast workload in the Portal?

* **Daily attendance** shows the attendance rate and the number of **Present**, **Partially absent**, and **Absent** workers.
* **Daily activity** shows **Scheduled appointments** and **Completed appointments**, first for all activity, then by intervention type or group.
* **Forecast workload for the next 3 weeks** is a weekly bar chart. Hover over a week to see **Hourly capacity**, **Forecast workload**, and **Delta**.
