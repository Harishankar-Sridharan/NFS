# How do I add an unavailability from the Availabilities schedule?

1. Hover over a resource's time slot. A window shows the **Available working hours** and the day template.
2. Select the **Add an unavailability** icon.
3. Enter the **Type**, **Duration**, and dates. The resource is filled in for you.
4. Select **OK**, or **Cancel** to close without creating it.
