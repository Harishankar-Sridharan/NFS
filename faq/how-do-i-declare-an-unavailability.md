# How do I declare an unavailability?

1. Go to **Planning > Planning menu > Manage unavailabilities**.
2. In the **General** section, select the unavailability **Type** and the **Resource**.
3. Choose a **Duration**: **One day**, **Full days**, **Morning**, **Afternoon**, or **Flexible schedule**. Then enter the **From** and **To** dates and times.
4. For a repeating unavailability, open **Recurrence**, enter **Every** with a number and a unit (**days**, **weeks**, or **months**), and enter an end date.
5. Optionally, enter an **Address** and **Comments**. Then select **Add unavailability**.
