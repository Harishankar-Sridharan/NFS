# Manage subcontractors, equipment and products

These entries describe the people and items related to interventions: subcontractors that can receive an intervention, required equipment, and delivered or affected products.

## Access the feature

| Entry          | Navigation                                                        | List filters                      |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Subcontractor  | `Administration > Company data > Subcontractor`                   | Region                            |
| Equipment      | `Administration > Company data > Equipment`                       | Region, Work site                 |
| Product family | `Administration > Company data > Product family > Product family` | Region                            |
| Product        | `Administration > Company data > Product family > Product`        | Region, Work site, Product family |

Each page provides a **Create…** button in the upper-right corner. Manage records using the [shared Administration principles](administration-overview.md).

## Subcontractor

A subcontractor record has **Information** and **Contact** tabs.

The **Information** tab contains:

* **Name**\*, **Abbreviation**, **Region**\*, **Work site**, **Resource type** and **Description**;
* the **Address** section: **Address**, **Address line 2**, **Postcode**\*, **City**\*, **Country**, **Original input** and **X, Y coordinates**.

Use **Validate address**, **Map** and **Street View** to check the location.

![Subcontractor record, Information tab](/broken/files/ydQ3tURhbBKUBDRbZC7L)

## Equipment

An equipment record has one page:

* **Name**\*, **Abbreviation**, **Region**\*, **Work site**, **Description**;
* **Duration**, **Stock**, **Stock2**, **Load**, **Load2**;
* **Number of employees** and **Priority**.

![Create equipment](/broken/files/6yJXP0pqBwbdf91MYfnu)

## Product family

A product family groups products. Its record contains **Name**\*, **Abbreviation**, **Region**\* and **Description**.

## Product

A product record uses the equipment fields and adds:

* **Family**\*: the related product family;
* **Unit price**.

![Create a product](/broken/files/QOzrx0WwniNrt3BFNarL)

{% hint style="info" %}
Create the product family before the product. The **Family** field is required.
{% endhint %}
