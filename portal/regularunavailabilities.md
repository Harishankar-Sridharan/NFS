# Regular unavailabilities

## Overview

The regular unavailabilities table on the [Unavailabilities](unavailabilities.md) page shows the regular unavailabilities of the resource. A regular unavailability is repeated at regular intervals over a period of time.

For each unavailability, the table displays:

* **Start date and time**
* **End date and time**
* **Periodicity**
* **Repeat-until date** – the date up to which the unavailability is repeated
* **Unavailability type**

## Sort, edit or delete

Sorting the list, deleting an unavailability and editing its notes work the same way as for one-off unavailabilities. See [One-off unavailabilities](oneoffunavailabilities.md) for the steps (click a column heading to sort, **Delete**, **Edit Notes**, **Save**, **Back**).

To change the dates of a regular unavailability, use the [unavailability form](unavailabilityform.md).
