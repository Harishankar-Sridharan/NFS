# Adding a multi-resource unavailability

## Overview

An unavailability of the **Meeting** or **Training** type can assign several resources. This is called a multi-resource unavailability.

## Add a multi-resource unavailability

1. In the **Unavailability Type** list of the [unavailability window](addinganunavailability.md), select **Meeting** or **Training**.
2. In the **Resource** list, choose the name of a resource concerned by the unavailability.
3. Click the ![Add button](../.gitbook/assets/bouton-add.png) button to select other resources.
4. To filter the list of resources faster, use the **resources**, **teams** and **profiles** radio buttons.
5. Choose the meeting or training you need in the list of existing meetings. You can also create a new meeting.
6. Specify the dates, times, periodicity, location and comments as described in [Adding an unavailability](addinganunavailability.md).
7. Click **Add Unavailability** to save the new unavailability.

A newly created meeting is saved and added to the list of existing meetings.
