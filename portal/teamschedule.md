# Team Schedule

## Overview

This page shows the team planning.

* To open this page, click **Team schedule** in the main menu. The resources displayed depend on your access rights. Depending on your rights profile, you can view your own planning, your team's planning, or the planning of all teams.
* The team planning shows appointments for the next five days from the current date. It shows appointments and unavailabilities for each resource in the team.
* To display a planning legend, click the **Legend** button in the main menu (see [Legend](legend.md)).
* The agendas are displayed in much the same way as in the Planning module (see [The Planning](../planning/theplanning.md)).
* As in the Planning module, you can navigate in the agenda (see [The Planning](../planning/theplanning.md)). You can also display the appointment form (see [Appointment Form](appointmentform.md)) or an unavailability form (see [Unavailabilities](unavailabilities.md)) by clicking the number beside it in the list.
* Click the ![Information button](../.gitbook/assets/bouton-informations.png) button to display the summarized form for the resource (see [The Planning](../planning/theplanning.md)).
