# Unavailabilities

## Overview

The **Unavailabilities** page lets you view, add, edit, or delete unavailabilities for a resource.

You can access the page from the **Portal** or **Planning** module, depending on the configuration. To open it, click **Unavailabilities** in the main menu.

An unavailability is a period during which a resource cannot perform an appointment. Unavailabilities are either one-off or regular:

* A [one-off unavailability](oneoffunavailabilities.md) applies to a specific period, such as a day of sick leave, time off in lieu, or a meeting.
* A [regular unavailability](regularunavailabilities.md) is repeated at regular intervals over a period of time.

Each unavailability is defined by a **type**, the **resource** concerned, a **date**, a **time window**, and a **location**. Some unavailability types have a default location configured for the resource. For example, the default location for a day of time off in lieu is the home address of the resource.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If an unavailability occurs while an appointment is planned for the same time window, the appointment is suspended.
{% endhint %}

## The Unavailabilities page

The page is divided into three sections:

![Unavailabilities page with the one-off list, the regular list and the management section](../.gitbook/assets/indisponibilite_3.png)

1. The upper section lists the next one-off unavailabilities for the resource. See [One-off unavailabilities](oneoffunavailabilities.md).
2. The middle section lists the regular unavailabilities for the resource. See [Regular unavailabilities](regularunavailabilities.md).
3. The lower section manages unavailabilities. See [Adding an unavailability](addinganunavailability.md).
