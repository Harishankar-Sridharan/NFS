# Imports and exports overview

CSV files enable data exchange between Nomadia Field Service and other tools.

## Access the feature

**Navigation:** `Administration > CSV Files`

The **CSV Files** group provides the following entries: **Import a CSV file**, **Export a CSV file**, **Semi-automatic import**, **Global CSV import/export**, **CSV import/export template**, **Traffic listeners**, **Predefined export links**, **Specific files**, and **XML files**.

## In this section

* [Export a CSV file](export-a-csv-file.md)
* [Import a CSV file](import-a-csv-file.md)

Other screens also provide CSV export. These include appointment search (**Export to CSV**) and several analyses in the **Supervision** module (**Export CSV**).
