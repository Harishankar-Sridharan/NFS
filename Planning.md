# Planning - Opti-Time Reference Guide

## Overview

The Planning section allows you to view and manage your scheduled appointments and time slots. This page helps you organize your calendar and plan your activities efficiently.

## Key Features

- View your upcoming appointments
- Schedule new appointments
- Manage your time slots
- Filter appointments by date or category

## Planning Interface

### Main View

The Planning page displays your calendar with all scheduled appointments. You can see appointments organized by date and time. The main interface provides an easy-to-navigate calendar view where all your scheduled activities are clearly visible.

### Features in Detail

#### 1. View Upcoming Appointments

Display all your scheduled appointments in chronological order. This view helps you stay on top of your schedule and ensures you don't miss any important events. Appointments are listed with their date, time, and relevant details.

#### 2. Schedule New Appointments

Create new appointments using the appointment form. Refer to [Appointment Form](Appointment%20form.md) for detailed instructions. Simply click the "Schedule New Appointment" button to begin creating a new event.

#### 3. Manage Time Slots

View and manage your available time slots for scheduling. This feature allows you to see which times are free and which are already booked, making it easier to find suitable times for new appointments.

#### 4. Filter Appointments

Filter your appointments by date range or category to find specific events quickly. Use the filtering options to narrow down your view and focus on the appointments you need to see.

## How to Use Planning

1. Navigate to the **Planning** section from the main menu
2. View your current appointments on the calendar
3. Use the filter options to narrow down appointments by date or category
4. Click on any appointment to view or edit details
5. Use the "Schedule New Appointment" button to create a new appointment
